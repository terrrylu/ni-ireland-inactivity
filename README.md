# Economic inactivity: Northern Ireland vs the Republic of Ireland

Independent research project by Terry (Tianyi) Lu, Northeastern University (N.U.in at Queen's University Belfast), advised by Dr. Anh Pham.

**Question:** Why is economic inactivity higher in Northern Ireland than in the Republic of Ireland, and is it mainly about labour supply or labour demand?

## Contents
- `notebooks/01_load_and_clean.ipynb`: loads and cleans the NISRA and OECD/FRED data
- `notebooks/02_charts.ipynb`: makes Chart 1 (NI vs Ireland, 2009–2025) and Chart 2 (by sex, 2013–2023)
- `data/raw/`: original downloaded files
- `data/clean/`: cleaned tables used for the charts
- `charts/`: chart images

## Data sources
- NISRA, Labour Force Survey tables for Local Government Districts, 2009–2025
- NISRA, Women in Northern Ireland 2023
- OECD labour force participation rates via FRED (LRAC64TTIEA156N, LRAC64FEIEA156N, LRAC64MAIEA156N), based on the CSO Labour Force Survey
